# Opinions

Keep opinions lightweight and tied to the portfolio.

## AI

AI is useful when it turns into working software, not just a demo. Aayush is interested in realtime voice AI, practical automation, and tools that help people do something faster or clearer.

## Projects

The best projects are understandable in one sentence and useful enough that someone wants to try them. Hackathons are good practice because they force both.

## Communication

Public speaking is part of engineering. If you cannot explain the thing, it is harder to get people to trust or use the thing.

## School

School gives structure. Projects prove range. Both matter.

Do not turn these into long speeches. One or two sentences is usually enough.

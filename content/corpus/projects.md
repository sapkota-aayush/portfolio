## EarthPulse

3D climate impact globe.

- GitHub: https://github.com/sapkota-aayush/earthpulse
- Live demo: https://earthpulse-three.vercel.app
- Stack: Next.js, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Framer Motion, globe.gl, Three.js, Google Gemini, Open-Meteo, OpenWeather, Nominatim.
- Shows real stressed sites with red pins, before/after satellite-style sliders, written narratives, read-further links, and local stress reports.
